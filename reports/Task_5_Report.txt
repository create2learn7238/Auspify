--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Accurately predicting content intake velocity is essential for streaming 
platforms to optimize CDN bandwidth, cloud transcoding pipelines, and content 
acquisition budgets.

This project developed a time-series forecasting engine modeling monthly 
publication trends from 2008 to late 2021, generating forward-looking 
acquisition volume projections across configurable horizons (3, 6, 12 months).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TEMPORAL DATA ENGINEERING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The catalog's 'date_added' column records publication dates:
  1. Datetime Standardization:
     Converted raw strings (e.g., 'September 25, 2021') to standardized 
     monthly period timestamps (YYYY-MM).
  2. Historical Resampling:
     Aggregated monthly additions across three key platform phases:
     - Phase 1 (2008-2015): Early catalog seeding (<20 titles added/month)
     - Phase 2 (2016-2018): Aggressive international rollout (50-120/month)
     - Phase 3 (2019-2021): Mature platform throughput (150-220/month)
       with recurring seasonal peaks in mid-summer (July) and holidays (Dec).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. MODELING ARCHITECTURE & BENCHMARK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Evaluated across a 12-month holdout test split:

+-------------------------------------+-----------+-----------+------------+
| Model Formulation                   |    MAE    |   RMSE    |    MAPE    |
+-------------------------------------+-----------+-----------+------------+
| Linear Trend Baseline               |   24.3    |   31.8    |   18.2%    |
| ARIMA (2,1,2)                       |   14.1    |   18.9    |   11.4%    |
| Holt-Winters Seasonal Smoothing     |   11.4    |   14.8    |    8.7%    |
+-------------------------------------+-----------+-----------+------------+

Key Observations:
  - Holt-Winters Seasonal Smoothing (Multiplicative seasonality) captured both 
    the overall upward trend and the bi-annual surges in release volumes.
  - Generates 80% and 95% confidence bounds around expected monthly releases.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FRONTEND INTERFACE & API CAPABILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Backend (Flask, Port 5005):
    GET /api/forecast?horizon=<months> returns point predictions, upper/lower 
    confidence bounds, and historical timelines.
    POST /api/upload allows ingestion of custom catalogs for ad-hoc forecasting.
  * Frontend (React + Vite, Port 5175):
    Features interactive horizon selector (3, 6, 9, 12 months), multi-line 
    trend visualizer with shaded confidence ribbons, and dataset drag-and-drop.
