1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Content catalog discovery is essential for streaming platforms to optimize 
licensing decisions, understand catalog diversity, and balance content 
inventories across formats and geographies.

This project implements an end-to-end unsupervised segmentation pipeline 
combining K-Means Clustering with Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to 
uncover organic content clusters across Netflix's catalog without human 
supervision.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FEATURE SPACE CONSTRUCTION & STANDARDIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Titles were transformed into a dense feature representation combining both 
numerical measurements and multi-hot categorical vectors:

  1. Z-Score Feature Standardization:
     Duration and release year were standardized to zero mean and unit variance:
         z = (x - mean) / std
     This ensures release year (~2000) does not dominate Euclidean distance 
     metrics over runtime duration (~100).
  2. Multi-Hot Genre Vectorization:
     Extracted binary indicators across the platform's leading 20 genre tags.
  3. Regional Origin Flags:
     Encoded binary flags for the dominant global production hubs:
     United States, India, United Kingdom, Japan, and South Korea.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CLUSTERING ALGORITHM & EVALUATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Algorithm: K-Means Clustering
   Iteratively minimizes the within-cluster sum of squares (WCSS / Inertia):
       J = sum_{i=1..k} sum_{x in S_i} ||x - mu_i||^2

B. Cluster Selection & Silhouette Analysis:
   Tested cluster counts k from 3 to 8. The elbow inflection point and 
   silhouette score peaked at k = 5 with an average score of 0.42:

   +-----------+---------------+------------------------------------------+
   | Cluster k | Silhouette    | Cluster Separability                     |
   +-----------+---------------+------------------------------------------+
   |   k = 3   |     0.31      | High overlap between film categories     |
   |   k = 4   |     0.37      | Moderate cluster cohesion                |
   |   k = 5   |     0.42      | Optimal separation across catalog types  |
   |   k = 6   |     0.39      | Fragmented sub-clusters formed           |
   +-----------+---------------+------------------------------------------+

C. PCA Dimensionality Reduction:
   Reduced the multi-dimensional feature space to 2 orthogonal principal 
   components (PC1, PC2) via Singular Value Decomposition (SVD):
   - PC1: Captures runtime duration & content format (Movies vs TV Series).
   - PC2: Captures cultural & regional production divergence (Western vs Asian).
