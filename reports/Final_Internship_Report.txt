1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This report summarizes the engineering, modeling, and deployment of four machine 
learning applications using the Netflix Movies and TV Shows dataset (~8,800 titles):

  1. Task 3: Audience Rating Classification (Supervised Random Forest)
  2. Task 4: Content Segmentation (Unsupervised K-Means + PCA)
  3. Task 5: Trend Forecasting Model (Time-Series Seasonal Smoothing)
  4. Task 6: Content Analytics & Success Engine (Full-Stack Intelligence Hub)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. STRATEGIC BUSINESS TAKEAWAYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TV Show Pivot: TV series additions increased by >300% post-2016, proving

   segments, essential for international subscriber acquisition.
3. Adult Audience Dominance: Over 61% of catalog titles fall under TV-MA and TV-14, 
   highlighting the importance of automated parental control systems.
